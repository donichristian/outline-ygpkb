=> A naive `await import("@tanstack/ai")` compiles back to `require("@tanstack/ai")` and fails too.
   `module.ignoreDynamic:true` keeps the import but changing .swcrc is out of scope (MUST NOT).
   Workaround used: assemble the import from a string — `new Function("specifier","return import(specifier);")`.

DECISION: Static ESM import (and a plain dynamic import) CANNOT work under the repo's swc
CommonJS transform. The interop mechanism MUST be a string-assembled dynamic `import()`
(the kept loaders in server/routes/api/ai/tanstack.ts).
