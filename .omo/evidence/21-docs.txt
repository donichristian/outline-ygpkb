## 5. What the docs now assert

- The wire is AG-UI over SSE: `data: {json}` frames, no `event:` field, no
  provider sentinel, terminal `RUN_FINISHED` or `RUN_ERROR`.
- `RUN_ERROR` with the adapter code `incomplete-stream` maps to
  `status: "truncated"`; other errors map to `error`; aborts to `aborted`.
- Refusal and busy are synthetic five-event runs discriminated only by
  `X-AI-Chat-Kind: refusal|busy`.
- `threadId` is the persisted session id; the server resolves
  `body.sessionId ?? body.threadId` and mints its own `runId`.
- The frozen read-only `search_workspace` allowlist is the only tool source;
  client-supplied `tools` and `forwardedProps` are ignored.
- `AI_CHAT_REASONING` reaches the provider as `reasoning_effort` through
  `modelOptions`; thinking is live-only in the UI.
- `whenBusy` (`queue` / `drop` / `interrupt`) lives in panel state, with
  per-item cancel and busy-text restore.
- The in-flight lock TTL is 900s, justified by the tool-loop worst case
  (about 5 rounds x (120s model + 5s tool) = 625s, plus a spare round and the
  terminal flush).
- The old stateless verbatim-passthrough guarantee is retired.
- The Koa route reads `chatParamsFromRequestBody(ctx.request.body)` because
  `koa-body` already parsed the body.
- Slice 2 is marked implemented, with the deferred list intact: subagents,
  resumable streams, TanStack persistence, client tools, token usage.
